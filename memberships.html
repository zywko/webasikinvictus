Rules and Visit Checklist

What you must bring to the gym

Pack your gym bag carefully before leaving home:

  • Clean indoor shoes (dirty street sneakers are not allowed)
  • Sportswear:
    • T-shirt or hoodie
    • Shorts or track pants
  • Personal shower towel
  • Charged smartphone to scan at turnstiles

Steps for your first workout

  1. Download the Invictus GO app on your phone.
  2. Add your bank card and pick the student or standard plan.
  3. Come to Mangilik El 18 and scan your QR code at the gate.
  4. Choose an empty locker and tap it with your app.
  5. Wipe the bench after finishing your exercise set.

Important terms to remember

QR Pass
A dynamic code in the phone app that acts as your electronic key for the door.
Free Weight Zone
Area with barbells and dumbbells where you must return plates back to their racks.
Guest Pass
A single-entry ticket you can gift to a friend once per monthly subscription.
