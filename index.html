About this branch

I go to Invictus GO on Mangilik El 18. This spot is popular in Astana because it has no reception desk or paper tickets. You just install their app, buy a plan, scan your personal QR code at the gate, and step inside. The lockers also unlock with the same phone app, so you never carry metal keys around.

The gym has plenty of bench presses, dumbbells from 2 to 50 kg, and cardio treadmills. The air conditioner keeps the room at about 19°C, so it never feels too hot. They also put water taps near the locker rooms, so you can fill your own water bottle for free.

My opinion and gym schedule

Training here as a student

I like this place because trainers do not walk up to sell you classes. You just put your headphones on and do your sets. My workouts take about 60-75 minutes. The only issue is the evening crowd. Around 20:00 it gets very busy, and you have to wait for the bench or cable machines.

I asked a guy named Daniyar why he picks this gym:

"It is close to my home, cheap enough, and open all night. I just finish my sets and walk out."

Daniyar, 21, chat in the locker room on September 13, 2026.
